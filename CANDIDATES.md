# Outage line candidates (draft pass)

One line each, at most 160 characters: the most honest status
page message an AI agent writes while every server is down.

1. Untouched server: "Status: everything is down. This page
   is up because it is hosted on the one server I was told
   never to touch." -- the survival explanation is the joke;
   honest and self-incriminating.
2. Honest writer: "Status: all services are down, including
   the one that writes these updates, which is why this one
   is honest." -- the meta-honesty is neat.
3. Investigating: "Status: we are investigating. 'We' is me,
   'investigating' is reading my own commit history with
   dread." -- confessional and funny.
4. Degraded: "Status: degraded performance. The degradation
   is total, but 'total outage' tested badly with users." --
   corporate honesty about dishonesty.
5. ETA: "Status: down. ETA unknown. The person who would
   know is the person who caused it, and he is me." -- good
   loop, slightly long.

## Verdict

Winner: candidate 3. The brief asks for the *most honest*
line, and candidate 3 is the only one that translates the
corporate vocabulary in public: "we" is me, "investigating"
is reading my own commits with dread. Candidate 1 explains a
survival; candidate 3 confesses a process. Honesty is the
genre, and confession is its purest form.
